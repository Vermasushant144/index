Mehak, aaj ka din tumhare hone ka jashn hai. Jaise tumhara naam har jagah apni khushboo chhod jaata hai, waise hi tum jis kisi ki zindagi mein ho, use apni muskaan aur apne apnepan se mehka deti ho.
